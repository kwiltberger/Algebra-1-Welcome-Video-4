Introduction Week

How to Schedule a Teacher Check-In

Ask questions, review skills, and get support before completing major assignments.

Why Schedule a Check-In?

A Teacher Check-In gives you live support.

❓ Ask Questions Get help with something that is confusing.
📝 Review Skills Practice important concepts with your teacher.
✅ Check Readiness Make sure you are prepared for Prove It! or an assessment.
📅 Choose a Time That Works
1

Open the Scheduling Link

Use the Teacher Check-In page in Canvas and select the scheduling button.

Open Teacher Scheduling Calendar

Choose Your Appointment

Schedule a time you know you can attend.

  1. Review the available dates and appointment times.
  2. Select a time that does not conflict with another class or responsibility.
  3. Enter your correct name and school email address.
  4. Submit the appointment and wait for confirmation.

After Scheduling

Check your email and calendar.

Confirmation Email Make sure the appointment was successfully scheduled.
Meeting Link Save the link you will use to join the live meeting.
Appointment Time Add the correct date and time to your calendar.

Prepare for the Meeting

Bring what you need to use the time well.

Bring Your Materials

Have your notes, assignments, calculator, and any problems you want to discuss ready before the meeting.

Prepare Your Questions

Write down specific questions or examples so your teacher knows exactly where you need support.

  • Join the meeting on time.
  • Use a quiet place when possible.
  • Be ready to explain what you have tried.
  • Participate and ask questions.
2

Reschedule When Necessary

If you cannot attend, change or cancel your appointment as early as possible so the time can become available to another student.

Do not simply miss the appointment. Use the calendar confirmation to reschedule, or contact your teacher before the meeting time.

Ask for Help Early

You do not need to wait until you are completely stuck.
